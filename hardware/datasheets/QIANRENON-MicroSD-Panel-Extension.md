## Intended use

The extender relocates the Waveshare board's onboard microSD/TF slot to the
rear panel. The flexible ribbon must route without a sharp crease, rubbing edge,
or tension on the board socket. Support the external receptacle with its bulkhead
nut and O-ring; do not use the internal card-shaped plug or ribbon as strain
relief.

This component creates the third rear-panel port, alongside NMEA 2000 and USB-C.
The existing 3 mm printed panel is within the listing's 6.3 mm maximum thickness,
but the current two-port panel and 64 × 26 mm rear-cover opening were not designed
for three connector flange/nut envelopes. Redesign the opening and replaceable
panel only after measuring all three received connectors.

## Receipt checks

Before final CAD or exposed boat use, measure and record:

- actual thread major diameter and required print clearance
- flange, cap, nut and O-ring outside diameters
- threaded grip length and usable panel-thickness range
- inward projection, ribbon exit direction and minimum comfortable bend radius
- clearance needed to insert/remove the card and unscrew the cap
- continuity and card read/write operation through the extender
- sealing contact and cap/O-ring fit

The IP67 statement is a product-listing claim, not an enclosure-system rating.
The completed panel, printed material, gasket, fasteners and installation still
require leak testing. Power down or have firmware unmount the card before removal.
